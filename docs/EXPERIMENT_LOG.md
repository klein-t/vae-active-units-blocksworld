- **Literature check (2026-10-27, post hoc; no verdict changes).** A post-results literature search bears on three readings of this run:
  1. **Learning rate.** In Asperti & Trentin (2002.07514, §5, Table 5), a higher initial learning rate leaves *more* latents active: 13 to 19 inactive of 64 as lr falls from 2e-4 to 5e-5, in one CIFAR-10 setup with no seeds. The single cell that moves here (A2 lr 3e-3: 10 units instead of 15) goes the other way. Agreement with [2] holds for sensitivity, not direction. Their inactivity criterion (mean posterior variance >= 0.8) also differs from ours (Burda, Var > 0.01).
  2. **gamma floor.** The floor of the learned gamma on `b_lin` is expected: a learned decoder variance tracks the reconstruction error, and the Gaussian likelihood is unbounded on a finite point set that the decoder reconstructs almost exactly (Mattei & Frellsen 2018). H2.4 therefore compared gamma ~ 1.2e-4 with gamma = 1.
  3. **Post-hoc spectral check (not a finding).** Under the uniform weighting of the 866 states, the Blocksworld covariance has 16 eigenvalues >= 0.173, then 9 at 0.117-0.122. Under the train-split frequencies the VAEs were trained on, there is no gap after the 16th: 10 eigenvalues at 0.28-0.42, 10 at 0.115-0.127, 5 at 0.094-0.096. The linear eigenvalue-threshold theory therefore does not single out 15-16 on our training data.
